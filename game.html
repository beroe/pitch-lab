Microphone and speaker settings

The automatic noise gate listens to the room for a second before each game and sets the gate 12 dB above it. Notes are timed from when their sound starts; the detection delay (the microphone's own lag) is subtracted from that. The results show your average timing. If the notes reach the red line before you hear the click, raise the speaker delay until they match (it can be changed while playing, and is remembered on this device).
